01 / About me

The person behind the work

I make room
for feeling.

Lil Dycey is a contemporary artist working across painting, collage, and immersive installation. Their practice follows the emotional afterimage of everyday life: the flash of a sign, a song through a wall, the color of a room after everyone leaves.

Every piece begins with an instinct and stays open long enough to become something else. The result is work that feels immediate, layered, and entirely alive.
